The BNR mission.

Our mission is simple, build you the GT-R you've always dreamt of,
fully road-legal and insurable.

BNR Motorsports is a Gold Coast, Queensland based team that lives and breathes Skylines, built around one mission, your dream GT-R. We consult on your goals and your state's rules, carry out the work in-house with full documentation, and hand the car back in fully engineerable form.

Whether it's your first GT-R, or the build you've been planning for a decade, we're here to turn that plan into a car that's fully engineered and documented, ready for your state's certifier to complete registration so you can drive and enjoy it.
